Hair Care Guide

Everything you need to keep your Rose extensions looking gorgeous for years to come.

Wash Routine

The Rose Wash Method

Follow these steps for clean, hydrated, and long-lasting extensions.

1

Detangle Gently

Before wetting, use a wide-tooth comb or detangling brush to remove any knots, starting from the ends and working your way up. Never brush from root to tip on dry hair.

2

Shampoo with Care

Use a sulfate-free, moisturizing shampoo. Dilute in water for even distribution. Gently massage the scalp area and let the suds run through the lengths. Avoid scrubbing the mid-lengths and ends.

3

Deep Condition

Apply a hydrating deep conditioner from mid-shaft to ends. Leave for 10-15 minutes under a plastic cap for deeper penetration. For extra moisture, use a steamer or warm towel method.

4

Rinse & Seal

Rinse thoroughly with cool water to seal the cuticles. Apply a lightweight leave-in conditioner and an oil or serum to the ends for moisture retention and shine.

5

Air Dry or Low Heat

Allow hair to air dry whenever possible to maintain the natural texture pattern. If using heat, always apply a heat protectant and use low-to-medium settings. Diffuse curly textures on low speed.

Daily Care

Maintenance Tips

Sleep Protection

Wrap your hair in a silk or satin bonnet every night. Alternatively, sleep on a silk pillowcase to reduce friction and prevent tangling while you rest.

Moisture Is Key

Raw hair thrives on moisture. Use a lightweight oil like argan or jojoba 2-3 times per week on the mid-lengths and ends. Avoid heavy products that cause buildup.

Minimize Heat

While our raw hair can handle heat styling, minimizing heat exposure will extend the lifespan significantly. Embrace the natural texture and save heat styling for special occasions.

Color Guide

Coloring Your Rose Hair

Our raw hair is a perfect canvas for coloring. Because it is completely unprocessed, it accepts color beautifully and evenly. Here are some tips for the best results:

Always have a professional colorist handle bleaching sessions. Use a 20 or 30 volume developer for best results. The hair can be lifted to a 613 blonde with proper technique. After coloring, deep condition immediately and follow up with weekly moisture treatments.

For semi-permanent or temporary color, no developer is needed. Simply apply and process according to the product instructions. The natural dark brown base is perfect for rich burgundy, chocolate, and auburn tones.
