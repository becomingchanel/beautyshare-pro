Hair Care Guide

Caring for Your Crown

Proper care is the secret to getting 3-5 years of beautiful wear from your raw bundles. Follow our expert guide below.

Wash Day

The 3-Step Wash Routine

Keep your bundles looking fresh and beautiful with our recommended wash routine.

1

Detangle & Pre-Treat

Before wetting the hair, gently detangle from ends to roots using a wide-tooth comb. Apply a lightweight oil to the ends to prevent dryness. This step minimizes tangling and shedding during the wash process.

2

Cleanse Gently

Use a sulfate-free, pH-balanced shampoo. Work the shampoo in a downward motion from roots to ends. Never scrub in circular motions as this can cause tangling. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

3

Condition & Seal

Apply a hydrating conditioner from mid-length to ends. Leave on for 5-10 minutes, then rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle. Follow with a leave-in conditioner and air dry whenever possible.

Maintenance Tips

Follow these tips to extend the life of your raw bundles and keep them looking like new.

  • Deep condition at least once a week to maintain moisture and elasticity
  • Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce friction and prevent dryness
  • Always use a heat protectant spray before flat ironing or curling
  • Avoid using products with sulfates, parabens, or heavy silicones
  • Detangle gently from ends to roots using a wide-tooth comb or your fingers
  • Wash your bundles every 7-10 days, or more frequently if using heavy products
  • Store unused bundles in a silk bag or wrap to prevent tangling and dust buildup
  • Limit heat styling to 350-400 degrees Fahrenheit maximum
Advanced Care

Coloring Your Raw Hair

Can I Color My Noir Crown Bundles?

Absolutely. Because our hair is 100% raw and unprocessed with intact cuticles, it accepts color beautifully. You can lighten, tone, or deposit color just as you would with your natural hair.

Our Recommendations:

  • Always perform a strand test on a small section before coloring the full bundle
  • We strongly recommend having a licensed professional handle any lightening or bleaching
  • Use a bond-building treatment during the coloring process to maintain hair integrity
  • Deep condition immediately after coloring and continue weekly treatments
  • Avoid box dyes. Use professional-grade color formulations for the best and safest results
