Welcome to BeautyShare Pro

BeautyShare Pro is a dropship membership platform for hair entrepreneurs. You sell premium raw hair under your own brand. We fulfill every order from our US warehouse in your branded packaging. You never touch inventory.

What's Included in Your Membership

✦Wholesale access to all BSP raw hair products
✦Order fulfillment and tracking — fully automated
✦Weekly live webinars with Chanel — every Wednesday at 7PM CST
✦Free Boss Community on Discord
✦Profit & Stylist Calculator to determine your ideal retail prices
✦Training video library — full member access
✦BSP AI Assistant — 24/7 instant support powered by Claude AI

The BSP Model

✦You sell. Set your own retail price. Customer pays you directly.
✦We ship. BSP fulfills the order in your branded packaging within 1–2 business days.
✦You profit. You pay BSP's wholesale price. Everything above that is yours — no splits, no commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Use the BSP Assistant or submit a ticket.

How does pricing work — do you tell me what to charge?▼
No — you set your own retail prices completely. BSP has a fixed wholesale price for each product. You decide what to charge your customers. Whatever you charge above the wholesale price is your profit. Use the Profit Calculator in your dashboard to find the price point that works best for your market.
How does payment work — do I send you money first?▼
Your customer pays you at your retail price when they order. You then pay BSP the wholesale cost to fulfill the order. The difference is your profit — you keep it. There are no weekly payouts from BSP because you're collecting the money directly from your customers upfront.
How long does fulfillment take after a customer orders?▼
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days from our US warehouse. Shipping takes 2–5 additional business days. Your customer gets automatic tracking by email. Total delivery is typically 4–7 business days from order placement.
Will my customer know the hair came from BSP?▼
No. All orders ship in your branded packaging with your return label and business name. Your customers will never see any BSP branding. This is full white-label dropshipping — your brand is the only one they see.
What is the return and exchange policy?▼
BSP accepts returns within 7 days for unworn hair with original tags intact. Hair that has been installed, colored, cut, or chemically treated cannot be returned. Your customer contacts you → you submit the return in your dashboard → BSP processes the exchange.
How does the 24/7 AI Assistant work?▼
The BSP Assistant is powered by Claude AI — the same AI that helps major companies with customer service. It's loaded with all of BSP's policies, your product catalog, fulfillment rules, and return policy. When you ask a question, it responds instantly based on that knowledge — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with no human needed. For account-specific issues like a specific order problem, it routes you to submit a support ticket so a real team member can help.
What products does BSP carry?▼
BSP carries 100% raw human hair sourced from Southeast Asia and Cambodia. Products include: Raw SEA Wavy Bundles, Raw SEA Wavy/Curly Bundles, Cambodian Wavy Bundles, Raw Wavy Bulk Braiding Hair, Raw Curly Bulk Braiding Hair, Cambodian Wavy K-Tips (50g/100g/150g), and Cambodian Curly K-Tips (50g/100g). Available in lengths 16"–28". Use the Profit Calculator to see full pricing.
Is there a limit on how many orders I can place?▼
No order limit. Scale as big as you can — our warehouse is built for volume. Top Bosses process 100+ orders per month.
