The Ultimate
Hair Care Guide

Maximize the lifespan of your raw hair with our expert care routine. Follow these steps to keep your bundles looking salon-fresh for years.

Step by Step

The Perfect Wash Routine

Raw hair requires a gentle, sulfate-free wash routine to maintain its natural cuticle integrity.

1

Detangle First

Gently brush from ends to roots with a wide-tooth comb before wetting. This prevents matting and breakage during the wash process.

2

Cleanse Gently

Use a sulfate-free shampoo and work in downward strokes only. Never scrub in circular motions — this disrupts cuticle alignment.

3

Deep Condition

Apply a hydrating mask from mid-shaft to ends. Leave on for 15–30 minutes under a cap, then rinse with cool water to seal cuticles.

Daily Care

Maintenance Tips

  • 🌟

    Sleep Protection

    Always wrap your hair in a silk or satin bonnet before bed. This prevents friction, tangling, and moisture loss overnight.

  • 💧

    Hydration is Key

    Spritz with a light leave-in conditioner every 2–3 days. Raw hair thrives on moisture — don't let it dry out between washes.

  • ☀️

    Heat Protection

    Always use a heat protectant before flat ironing or curling. Keep temperature below 375°F to preserve cuticle health.

  • 💖

    Storage Matters

    When not wearing your hair, store in a silk bag away from dust and sunlight. Braid loosely to prevent tangling.

Advanced

Coloring Raw Hair

One of the biggest advantages of raw hair is that it can be colored and bleached just like your natural hair.

  • 1

    Strand Test First

    Always do a strand test before full application. Raw hair from different donors may lift differently.

  • 2

    Use Quality Products

    Invest in salon-grade developer and color. Box dye will damage the cuticle layer and shorten lifespan significantly.

  • 3

    Deep Condition After

    Always follow color processing with a protein treatment and deep conditioner to restore moisture balance.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

What’s the difference between raw and virgin hair? +
Raw hair is completely unprocessed — it hasn’t been steamed, chemically treated, or altered in any way. Virgin hair may still have been steam-processed to create uniform curl patterns. Our REIGN hair is 100% raw, meaning you get the most natural, long-lasting hair possible.
How long does raw hair last? +
With proper care, our raw hair bundles last 3–5 years. The key is following a consistent maintenance routine: sulfate-free washing, regular deep conditioning, silk bonnet protection at night, and minimal heat usage.
Can I bleach and color REIGN hair? +
Yes! Because our hair is raw and unprocessed with intact cuticles, it takes color beautifully — just like your natural hair. We recommend using a professional colorist and always doing a strand test first.
How many bundles do I need for a full install? +
For a full sew-in, we recommend 3 bundles + 1 closure. For 14”–20” lengths, 3 bundles is perfect. For 22”–28”, you may want 4 bundles for extra fullness. Each bundle weighs approximately 100g.
What’s your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day return policy on unaltered, unused hair with original packaging and tags intact. Once hair has been washed, colored, cut, or installed, it cannot be returned. Please inspect your bundles upon receipt.
