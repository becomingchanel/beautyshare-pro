Hair Education

Everything you need to know about raw hair textures, care, and styling.

Raw Wavy Texture

Our wavy texture offers a loose, beachy wave pattern that is incredibly versatile. It blends seamlessly with most natural hair types.

  • Natural loose wave pattern (2A-2B)
  • Minimal tangling — easy maintenance
  • Straightens beautifully with a flat iron
  • Returns to wave pattern after washing
  • Best for: everyday wear, professional settings, effortless glam

Raw Wavy/Curly Texture

The perfect middle ground — a defined S-wave with soft curls that offer volume and movement. Our most popular texture.

  • Defined S-curl pattern (2C-3A)
  • Beautiful volume and body
  • Can be worn as-is or styled straight
  • Pattern bounces back after every wash
  • Best for: full-bodied looks, natural blending, versatile styling

Raw Curly Texture

Bold, defined curls for those who love volume and statement looks. Tight, springy curl pattern that holds its shape beautifully.

  • Tight defined curls (3B-3C)
  • Maximum volume and drama
  • Blends perfectly with natural curly hair
  • Can be stretched or straightened
  • Best for: natural looks, bridal styles, high-fashion editorial

The Ultimate Hair Care Guide

Maximize the lifespan of your raw hair with our expert care routine. Follow these steps to keep your bundles looking salon-fresh for years.

Step by Step

The Perfect Wash Routine

Raw hair requires a gentle, sulfate-free wash routine to maintain its natural cuticle integrity.

1

Detangle First

Gently brush from ends to roots with a wide-tooth comb before wetting. This prevents matting and breakage.

2

Cleanse Gently

Use a sulfate-free shampoo and work in downward strokes only. Never scrub in circular motions.

3

Deep Condition

Apply a hydrating mask from mid-shaft to ends. Leave on for 15-30 minutes under a cap, then rinse with cool water.

Daily Care

Maintenance Tips

  • 🌟

    Sleep Protection

    Always wrap your hair in a silk or satin bonnet before bed to prevent friction and tangling.

  • 💧

    Hydration is Key

    Spritz with a light leave-in conditioner every 2-3 days. Raw hair thrives on moisture.

  • ☀️

    Heat Protection

    Always use a heat protectant before styling. Keep temperature below 375°F.

  • 💖

    Storage Matters

    When not wearing your hair, store in a silk bag away from dust and sunlight.

Advanced

Coloring Raw Hair

Raw hair can be colored and bleached just like your natural hair. Here is how to do it safely:

  • 1

    Strand Test First

    Always do a strand test before full application. Different donors may lift differently.

  • 2

    Use Quality Products

    Invest in salon-grade developer and color. Box dye will damage the cuticle layer.

  • 3

    Deep Condition After

    Always follow color processing with a protein treatment and deep conditioner.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

What is the difference between raw and virgin hair? +
Raw hair is completely unprocessed. Virgin hair may still have been steam-processed to create uniform curl patterns. Our REIGN hair is 100% raw for the most natural, long-lasting result.
How long does raw hair last? +
With proper care, our raw hair bundles last 3-5 years. The key is sulfate-free washing, regular deep conditioning, silk bonnet protection at night, and minimal heat usage.
Can I bleach and color REIGN hair? +
Yes! Because our hair is raw and unprocessed with intact cuticles, it takes color beautifully. We recommend using a professional colorist and doing a strand test first.
How many bundles do I need for a full install? +
For a full sew-in, we recommend 3 bundles + 1 closure. For 22-28 inch lengths, you may want 4 bundles for extra fullness. Each bundle weighs approximately 100g.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day return policy on unaltered, unused hair with original packaging and tags intact. Once hair has been washed, colored, cut, or installed, it cannot be returned.
